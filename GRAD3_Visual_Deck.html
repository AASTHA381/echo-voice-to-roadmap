Executive Summary
Strategic Frame: The SCQA Framework
S
Situation
India's premium derma sunscreen market is expanding at 22% CAGR, hitting ₹2,800 Cr in 2024. Consumers are migrating to derm-backed formulations.

The ₹400–800 price band represents 48% of top online premium listings.

78% say derm recommendation is their #1 purchase trigger.
›
C
Complication
Galderma commands only 1 SKU — Cetaphil Sun at ₹1,080 — missing the ₹400–800 sweet spot completely.

72% of consumers reject thick creams. No mist, serum, or tinted options available.

Competitors (Re'equil ₹695, Minimalist ₹399) are rapidly consolidating this gap.
›
Q
Question
How can Galderma leverage its 40+ years of derm equity and Cetaphil's heritage to intercept the ₹400–800 consumer segment before competitors lock it down?

How do we match modern format expectations at scale?
›
A
Answer
Launch a 5-SKU price ladder (₹449–₹999) under Cetaphil & Biluma.

✔ 5,000-derm seed network
✔ Q-comm 30-min GTM funnel
✔ SunMatch AI skin quiz widget
