Executive Summary: The SCQA Framework
SLIDE 1 · EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
S
SITUATION
India's premium derma sunscreen market is growing at 22% CAGR, reaching ₹2,800 Cr in 2024 (Euromonitor). Consumers aged 25–35 are rapidly shifting from mass brands to dermatologist-backed SPF solutions.

The ₹400–800 price band commands 48% of audited Nykaa/Amazon premium listings (N=50 audit).

Preventive skincare, anti-aging awareness & social media dermatology content are accelerating this shift.

78% of consumers say dermatologist recommendation is their #1 brand switch trigger.
›
C
COMPLICATION
Galderma India currently sells only 1 SKU — Cetaphil Sun Light Gel 50ml at MRP ₹1,080 — missing the ₹400–800 sweet spot entirely.

72% of surveyed consumers (N=500) reject thick, heavy-feel formats. No mist, serum, or tinted option exists.

Competitors are aggressively filling this gap: Re'equil ₹695, Minimalist ₹399, La Shield ₹780.

HCP sampling program is far below industry benchmark vs. IPCA & Re'equil derm outreach.
›
Q
QUESTION
How can Galderma leverage its unmatched 40+ years of dermatology equity and Cetaphil's 75-year skin heritage to capture India's fastest-growing SPF consumer segment (25–35 yrs)?

Specifically: How do we fill the ₹400–800 price gap before competitors consolidate it?

How do we match the format, texture, and channel expectations of modern Indian consumers?

How do we activate the dermatologist recommendation engine at scale?
›
A
ANSWER
Launch a 5-SKU derma portfolio ladder (₹449–₹999) backed by:

✔ 5,000-dermatologist HCP sampling program (top 20 cities)
✔ Omnichannel GTM: Nykaa → MT → Q-Commerce
✔ Brand territory: "Derm-Approved. India-Ready."
✔ SunMatch AI digital skin-quiz funnel

Projected Financial Impact:
₹40 Cr Year 1 → ₹350 Cr Year 5
₹840 Cr cumulative 5-year revenue
25% net contribution margin
Sources: Euromonitor India BPC 2024 [1] · Consumer Survey N=500 [2] · Nykaa/Amazon Audit N=50 [3]
